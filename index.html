SYNC ERP

A pharmaceutical field force management platform, in production at Medlife Pharmaceuticals.

SYNC covers what a pharma company actually runs on: attendance and GPS-verified doctor visits, expense claims, distributor sales, doctor investment and return, and a full double-entry finance module. The web client is React on Vite; the field app is React Native on Expo and works offline. Most of the logic sits in PostgreSQL — stored functions exposed as REST endpoints, triggers that enforce rules the client cannot bypass, and materialised views that keep dashboards fast.

  • Multi-tenant schema so one deployment serves several companies with isolated data
  • Offline-first attendance and call punching, with an on-device queue and idempotent sync
  • Hierarchy-aware sales aggregation that resolves in roughly 110 ms
  • Chart of accounts, general ledger, journal vouchers and trial balance, with sales auto-posting
  • React

Trav-Ease.pk

A trip planner for northern Pakistan, built around the road and the altitude.

Most travel sites treat the Karakoram like any other destination. Trav-Ease plans around the two things that actually derail a trip up there: whether the pass is open, and how fast you gain height. You choose a region and a length, and it builds a day-by-day itinerary with the elevation profile beside it, flagging any day that climbs faster than is comfortable.

  • Itinerary builder with a per-day altitude check against a safe daily gain
  • Route library filtered by maximum altitude, season and whether the road is currently open
  • Permits, stays and jeep hire attached to the legs that need them

  • Node.js
